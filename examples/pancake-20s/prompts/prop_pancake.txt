Clay stop-motion assets. Material: oil-based modelling clay, the surface showing tiny fingerprints, pinch marks and press marks; rounded, full shapes; real handmade objects photographed on a tabletop set, not 3D renders, not digital painting. The set has soft studio lighting with the key light from the upper left and soft shadows; standalone character and prop assets have even, flat light. Saturated, bright colours, mainly primaries such as cream yellow, tomato red, sky blue and grass green.

Prop asset. Transparent-background PNG: the background is fully transparent (alpha channel). No white background, no checkerboard, no ground, no cast shadow, no scenery of any kind.
Props are placed apart, not touching, all at the same scale. Each one is complete with plenty of empty space around it, never cut off by the frame edge or by another object. Use the same viewpoint as the shot the prop will be composited into: the camera is at the character's chest-to-eye height, slightly above props on the table or in hand, looking down about 15 degrees; not a top-down view. A prop that is also needed seen from above is listed separately and marked "top-down"; draw that one from above.
Props: two flat-lying clay props, spaced apart at the same scale: a round golden pancake with a soft uneven edge, and the same pancake flattened with a thumbprint and a few crumbs; eye-level slightly above, as if lying flat on a table
The opening of a pot, bowl, plate or similar container is a very flat horizontal ellipse (height about a quarter of the width), showing the back rim and a little of the inside. For a container that will hold something, make the inside a colour that contrasts strongly with the outside (e.g. a pale pot with a dark bottom). Anything lying flat on the table is drawn in an eye-level "lying flat" version: only its side and a sliver of its top surface are visible.

All characters and designs are original and must not resemble any existing animated character, toy or brand. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks or signatures anywhere in the image.
